## Decisions made

- Plain Node check scripts, no Vitest. `scripts/check-data.mjs` of the spec is split into `check-data-prices.mjs` and `check-data-calendar.mjs` (CHECK-6 A/B).
- Index convention verified against the daily-consumption export (opt-in demo-data check passed, TEMPO_DEMO_DATA=1).
- Counter/calendar mismatches are reported as warnings (count only); the check fails only above a 2% mismatch ratio.
- Boundary rule: all days between two readings straddling a contract change are excluded (not only one).
- Day view: hatch/dots overlays and segment strokes removed (illegible on sub-pixel bars) after the browser smoke test.
- Drop zone lives in `src/ui/dropzone.ts` (plan said the same); fixtures built in memory, `gen-fixtures.mjs` writes `fixtures/` on demand.
